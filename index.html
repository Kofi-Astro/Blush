The Designer

Precision is the softest luxury.

Blush Closet was founded on a simple belief: a garment — and the hair that frames it — should be engineered as carefully as they are imagined. Every silhouette starts as a hand-drafted pattern before a single seam is cut, and every unit of hair is hand-selected before it ever reaches an install chair.

"I don't just dress a client. I finish the whole look — from the hem to the hairline."

Today the atelier produces considered fashion collections alongside bespoke bridal work and a curated line of luxury ladies' hair — wigs, extensions, and closures sourced for texture, density, and durability. Fittings and installs are done in person, by appointment.
